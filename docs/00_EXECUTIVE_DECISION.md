# Executive Decision Record

## Decision

AegisEV will launch as a **Web/API exposure validation workbench**, not as a general-purpose autonomous pentest platform.

## Why

The original vision spans Web, Android, iOS, desktop binaries, WAF behavior, AI agents, RAG, reporting, and proxy management. That is a product portfolio, not a credible first product. The investor-grade wedge is evidence-backed Web/API validation and retest automation.

## Product promise

AegisEV helps authorized teams validate whether exposures are real, collect defensible evidence, generate remediation guidance, and verify fixes.

## Product anti-promise

AegisEV does not promise perfect security, AGI-level autonomous hacking, zero false positives, WAF bypass, stealth, ban evasion, or hands-free exploitation.

## First customer profile

- Small/medium AppSec teams.
- Security-conscious SaaS teams.
- Pentest consultants who need repeatable evidence and retest workflows.
- Internal security teams that cannot justify heavy enterprise platforms.

## Killer feature

**Fix verification:** after remediation, the product replays safe validation steps and reports whether the issue is still present, fixed, or inconclusive.
